# Privacy Policy

**Auto Refresher** is a browser extension developed by Shubham Dhamal.

## Information the extension uses

The extension uses browser tab information to identify the tab selected when you start auto-refresh or choose **Refresh now**. It reads the tab URL locally to avoid refreshing restricted browser pages and to apply any excluded-domain rules you configure.

Your preferences, including the refresh interval, enabled state, excluded domains, and selected tab ID, are stored in the browser's local extension storage.

## Information sharing

The extension does not send, sell, or share your browsing information or settings with the developer or any third party. It does not use analytics, advertising, or remote servers.

## Permissions

- **Tabs**: identify and reload the tab you selected.
- **Storage**: save your settings on your device.
- **Website access**: allow reloading the selected website tab.

## Data retention and control

Settings remain in your browser until you change them or remove the extension. You can clear the extension's stored data through your browser's extension settings or remove the extension to delete its local data.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the extension's behavior changes. For questions about this policy, contact Shubham Dhamal through the project's GitHub repository: <https://github.com/shubhamdhamal/loopback>.
